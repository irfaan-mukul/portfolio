ClearDoc

AI-powered customs compliance checker for shipping documents. Upload a commercial invoice, bill of lading, or packing list and get an instant compliance report with flagged issues and recommended fixes.

The Problem

Customs document errors are one of the most common causes of shipment delays in freight forwarding. A single incorrect HTS code, missing country of origin, or weight discrepancy can hold a shipment for days and cost thousands in fines. Most errors are caught at the border — after it's already too late.

The Solution

ClearDoc reviews documents before submission using AI that understands customs compliance requirements. It checks for invalid HTS codes, weight mismatches, currency inconsistencies, missing fields, address errors, and Incoterms conflicts — then produces a color-coded report with specific fix recommendations for each issue found.

FreightIQ

AI-powered air freight rate analyzer. Upload quotes from multiple carriers and get an instant side-by-side comparison with cost breakdown, flags, and a procurement recommendation.

The Problem

Freight forwarders receive rate quotes from multiple carriers in different formats — PDFs, emails, rate sheets — and manually comparing them is time-consuming and error-prone. Hidden charges and expiring quotes often get missed.

The Solution

FreightIQ reads quotes in any format, extracts all charges including hidden fees, normalizes them for comparison, flags expiring quotes and unusual surcharges, and recommends the best carrier based on cost, transit time, and risk.

RouteRisk

Real-time shipment delay predictor. Enter your route and get an AI-powered risk assessment based on live intelligence — current disruptions, carrier performance, port conditions, and geopolitical events.

The Problem

Freight forwarders often don't know a shipment is at risk until it's already delayed. By then it's too late to reroute, notify the customer, or find alternatives. Proactive risk intelligence before departure can prevent costly surprises.

The Solution

RouteRisk searches for live disruption data on your specific route — port congestion, carrier issues, weather events, geopolitical risks — then produces a color-coded risk score with contributing factors and delay estimates for both air and ocean freight.
